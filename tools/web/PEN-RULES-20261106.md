## Classic match: checked-in core rules

- Two families share a board. Swap neighbors to match three or more.
- Five rounds, two starting moves per turn, 20-second turns in the current local plan.
- Each family has three different grocery errands from three family members. Cleared groceries, including cascades, fill your errands. Completed cards are replaced and ready that member's family power.
- Match your own colored bolts/crests to fill Dad Mode: six in the current plan. Dad Mode is the first-person run; collected coins add points, and collected groceries clear corresponding groceries from the board and fill errands.
- Highest score after the last round wins; source tie-breaks are completed cards, then the family that opened the rounds.
- Normal 3-match clears; straight 4 creates a rocket; straight 5+ creates a rainbow; L/T creates a gift with a 3×3 blast. Rocket direction follows the swap with the current `RocketBySwap` setting.
- A 4+ from the initial player swap earns one extra move, up to **three extra-move grants per turn**. Lucky cascades and powers do not grant extra moves. A board without a legal move shuffles automatically.
- These rules do not establish live PvP availability. Keep public release/TestFlight/online claims tied to separate evidence.

## Current Sprint / Knockout direction

Four live players are the intended design; each plays **their own board**, with four full-body Dads racing above it. The board uses six Sprint-only items. No classic groceries/errands, family power-ups or Dad Mode powers. No persistent special tiles are left by combos: matches immediately become speed effects.

| Match shape | Effect in `Mgv8e`                            |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| 3           | Item's normal speed bonus for 3 s            |
| 4           | Twice the item's bonus for 3 s               |
| 5           | TURBO: +10 km/h for 6 s, independent of item |
| L/T         | SUPER BOOST: twice the item's bonus for 5 s  |
| Cascade     | +1 km/h per chain                            |

Item bonuses: drink +6, lightning +5, sneaker +4, water +3, orange +3, stopwatch +2 km/h. Stronger items are rarer. Examples apply the bonus once per match: three drinks = +6; four drinks = +12. Base 20, stacked cap 45 km/h; 400 m heat, roughly one minute. **All these numbers are explicitly provisional; marketing should emphasize “match to accelerate” instead of promising fixed balance.**

Current Knockout hub focus: **4→3→2→1 across three heats**, same surviving opponents and a fresh board each heat. Heat 1 eliminates fourth; Heat 2 eliminates third; final is 1v1. No lives/hearts system. The board rises after the fullscreen countdown and lowers for the finish. No-move reshuffle is automatic while the race continues. Eliminated players can watch the final or collect and leave. Rewards are coins + cups only; displayed 600/60, 300/30, 150/15, 50/5 are provisional. Event Sprint Cup and Qualify→Final remain alternate meta concepts, not additional confirmed launch modes.

## Puzzles, ranks and tournaments

**Puzzles (`UT2T5`):** one match win earns one previously unowned piece; loss/leave earns none. Home counts earned pieces, Puzzle Room counts placed pieces; pending earned pieces wait in the tray. Dragging near the right slot highlights it, correct drops snap, wrong drops return without penalty. Current worked example is 3×3, three pictures per room. Completing a picture allows one coin claim; finishing all three allows the room coin claim. Sunny Beach's example is 40 coins per puzzle and 100 for the room. No refill rewards. These are the design's worked example amounts, not universal economy guarantees.

**Ranks (`LuzU2`, selected C):** THIS WEEK / ALL TIME, plus one round country switch: globe=global, flag=player's country. Weekly global is a 30-family same-league group from mixed countries; weekly local includes that country's families across leagues. Country weekly resets Monday 00:00 UTC. All Time shows lifetime cups, global/local Top 100; no all-time reset. Remember filter choice per tab and keep your own sticky row. “Family of the Week” and rank rewards belong to the weekly league group, not the country-wide weekly view. Rewards shown are coins/cups only. Deployment not established by this Pen study.

**Tournaments (`XAQ3G`):** four families, two simultaneous semifinals, then the winners' final: two wins take the cup. Example event is DOUBLE DAD MODE, explicitly **your bolts fill Dad Mode twice as fast**, not a run lasting twice as long. Coins/cups only. Entry amounts, example prizes, round counts, leagues and the open bracket/presentation alternatives should not become fixed release promises.
