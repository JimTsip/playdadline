Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

DADLINE: Family Rush is made by THE LAST CLICK L.P., which is responsible for the data described here. The short version: the game never asks who you are, your progress lives on your phone with a copy on our server in the European Union so it survives a new device, and we do not sell or share anything with anyone.

Your progress

Your Dad, the family you name during onboarding, your coins, badges, career level, streak, missions and settings are stored on your phone, in the app's own private storage. That copy is the original; everything else is a mirror of it.

A copy is also kept on our server, so your Dad survives a reinstall or a new phone. What is stored there is the same progress listed above, a record of the coins you earn and spend, and the runs you complete — score, duration and checkpoints — which is how the game can tell a real run from a forged one. That copy sits on infrastructure in the European Union (Ireland).

The account, and what it is not

There is no sign-up here, no password, and you are never asked for an email address to play. The first time the game runs it gives itself an anonymous account: an identifier that means nothing outside this game and is not taken from your hardware. It is kept in your iPhone's keychain and exists so the copy of your progress on our server has something to belong to.

You may also sign in with Apple, and it is optional — the whole game plays without it. Its only purpose is to tie your progress to something that outlives the phone. Apple gives us an identifier specific to this app, and an email address only if you allow it; if you use Apple's Hide My Email, we never see your real one. We never receive your password or your Apple ID.

The leaderboard

If you appear on a leaderboard, other players see the display name you chose, your country and your score. Nothing else is published — not your email, not your identifier, not your family.

The country is read from your device's region setting. The game does not use GPS, does not ask for location permission, and does not look up where you are from your IP address. If you would rather not appear at all, do not enter a display name.

The public leaderboard is served through a Cloudflare worker that caches it briefly at the edge so the game stays fast. It carries the same three public fields and nothing more.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment: your card, your billing address and your Apple ID never pass through us and we never see them.

What reaches us is the receipt Apple issues — the transaction identifiers, the product bought, whether it came from the real store or Apple's sandbox, and when. We verify it with Apple and credit the coins. Keeping it is what stops the same purchase being credited twice, and what lets a refund take the coins back.

Ads

Some coins can be earned by watching a rewarded ad. Those ads are always your choice: nothing plays unless you tap to watch it, and the game is fully playable without ever watching one.

The ads are served by Google AdMob, which is the recipient of the data involved and may use device identifiers to select and measure them. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Google's list of ad partners. We do not receive an advertising profile about you from Google, and we do not give Google your progress, your family, your purchases or your email.

The game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so it hands Google no advertising identifier from your iPhone and the ads are not personalised through one.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows you Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and it keeps your answer. You can change it at any time from the privacy options inside the game.

Children

DADLINE is a game about family life, but it is made for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child. If you believe a child has used the game and left data with us, write to us and we will remove it.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDK of any kind is built into the game.
  • No push notifications, so no notification token is created or stored.
  • No location, no contacts, no photos, no microphone, no camera, no health data.
  • No advertising identifier is collected by us, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising or for anyone else's purposes.

Deleting everything

Write to hello@playdadline.com from the game and we will erase what we hold: the progress copy, the coin record, the runs, the leaderboard entry, and the anonymous account itself. We answer within 30 days.

Deleting the app removes the copy on your phone. If you signed in with Apple, you can also revoke it yourself from iPhone Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. The same address, hello@playdadline.com, reaches us for all of it.

We keep your progress for as long as the account is in use, and purchase records for as long as tax and consumer-protection law requires. You also have the right to complain to your national data protection authority; in Greece that is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and anything that materially affects you is announced in the game before it takes effect.
