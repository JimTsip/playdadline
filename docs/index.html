How a match plays

IT'S YOUR TURN

Two families, one board

Both families play the same 7×7 board, turn by turn: 5 rounds, 2 moves a turn, 20 seconds to play.

1 · MATCH 3

Clear pieces, fill your cards

Swap two pieces to line up 3. Whatever you clear fills your 3 errand cards: 2 points for every piece a card needs, and every piece charges the power-up of the family member who asked for it.

2 · MATCH 4

A Rocket and an extra move

4 in a row makes a Rocket and gives you an EXTRA MOVE. Every special you make charges a DAD MODE bolt.

3 · THE RACE

Finish a card: +10

Tap the Rocket to clear its line. Mom's card holds the race item, on both families' cards: finish it first for a second bolt.

4 · DAD MODE

Two bolts, one big move

DAD MODE clears the 2 columns holding most of what your cards still need. About once a match, so pick the moment.

5 · THE FAMILY HELPS

Mom's List Check

Each errand piece charged it, the finished card filled it. Mom grabs the pieces her card needs; Kid skates a row, the little one swaps stickers.

6 · ROUND 5

Final round: double points

Every point counts twice. Most points at the final buzzer wins the show.

7 · THE PRIZE

New studio unlocked

Wins grow your VIEWERS, and more VIEWERS open new TV studios, each with its own twist.

All the rules
  • Two families share one 7×7 board. 5 rounds; each round every family plays one turn of 2 moves, with 20 seconds. A coin flip picks who opens the rounds.
  • You hold 3 errand cards: 3 different items, each asked by a different family member, 5 or 6 pieces each. Everything your move clears, cascades included, fills them. A finished card is replaced straight away.
  • Points: 1 for any piece, 2 for a piece from your swap that fills your card, 1 for a lucky cascade piece, +10 for a finished card. At the end an open card more than half done pays 5.
  • The race: one item is on both families' cards. Finish it first for a DAD MODE bolt.
  • Specials: 4 in a row makes a Rocket, an L or T shape a Gift, 5 in a row a Rainbow. Move or tap one to fire it; two together make a bigger blast. A 4+ match or a special from your own swap gives an extra move, up to 3 a turn.
  • DAD MODE: every special your own move makes is a bolt. With 2 bolts, DAD MODE clears the 2 columns holding most of what your cards still need.
  • Power-ups: every piece you collect for a card charges the power-up of the family member who asked for it; it is ready when the card is finished. Mom's List Check collects up to 6 pieces, Kid's Skate Dash clears a row, the little one's Sticker Swap turns 5 pieces into the kind worth most to you. One power-up a turn; one free SHUFFLE a match.
  • In the final round every point counts double. Most points wins; a tie goes to more cards finished, then to the family that opened the rounds. A Dad who walks off loses.
  • VIEWERS are your progress: a win adds them, 3 wins in a row puts you ON FIRE for double, a loss takes 5, and you never drop below a studio you opened. Coins pay the ENTRY FEE; the winner takes the PRIZE.
