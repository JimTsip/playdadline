TONIGHT'S RUN OF SHOW

How to play

Seven things to know. Both families play the same rules on the same tiles: no family is stronger than another.

  1. 1

    Take turns on one board

    Both families play the same 7×7 board. A match has 5 rounds; each round every family plays one turn of 2 moves, with 20 seconds to play. A coin flip picks who opens the rounds.

    Swap two neighbouring pieces to line up 3 or more of a kind.

  2. 2

    Fill your errand cards

    You always hold 3 cards: three different items, each asked by a different family member. A card asks for 5 or 6 pieces, and both families get the same sizes.

    Everything your move clears, cascades included, fills your cards. Finish a card for +10 points and a new one takes its place straight away.

  3. RACE
    3

    Win the race item

    One item is always on both families' cards. That's the race. Finish it before the other family and you win a DAD MODE bolt.

  4. 4

    Make specials, earn moves

    • 4 in a row → Rocket. Flies the way you swiped and clears its line.
    • L or T shape → Gift. Blows up the pieces around it.
    • 5 in a row → Rainbow. Clears every piece of one kind.

    Move or tap a special to fire it; put two together for a bigger blast. A match of 4+ or a special from your own swap gives an EXTRA MOVE, up to 3 a turn.

  5. 5

    Charge DAD MODE

    Every special your own move makes is 1 bolt, and winning the race is another. With 2 bolts the Dad's buzzer is ready.

    Hit it and DAD MODE clears the 2 columns holding most of what your cards still need. About once a match, so pick the moment.

  6. 6

    The family helps

    Each card fills the power-up of the family member who asked for it. Finish the card and the power-up is ready. One power-up a turn.

    • Mom · List Check. Collects up to 6 pieces your nearest card needs.
    • Kid · Skate Dash. Clears a whole row.
    • Little one · Sticker Swap. Turns 5 pieces into the kind worth most to you.

    Stuck? SHUFFLE once a match. It doesn't cost a move.

  7. 7

    Finish strong

    In the FINAL ROUND every point counts double. At the final buzzer the family with most points wins.

    A tie goes to the family with more cards finished, then to the one that opened the rounds. A Dad who walks off loses the match.
