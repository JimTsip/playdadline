How a round plays

Tick off the errands

The groceries, the beach bag, the picnic basket. Swap and match items on the shared board before the other family does. Match 4 or more for an extra move.

Hit DAD MODE

Every match charges the Dad's buzzer. When it's full, one big move can turn the whole round.

The whole family helps

Mom, the kids and even the family pet each bring a power-up. Use them at the right moment and the family wins together.

Pick your family

Eight families, each with their own look, pet and personality. Same rules for all: no family is stronger than another.

Twelve TV studios

Each set brings its own items and its family crowd in the stands. Every studio has an ENTRY FEE in coins and the winner takes the PRIZE. The first studio is free.

Friendly by design

Families vs families, never mean. Every match is a fair duel on the same tiles, with the same items for both sides.
